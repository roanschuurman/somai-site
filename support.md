# Support

Email: **roan@aivenya.com**

We respond within 2 business days.

## Common topics

- **Restoring purchases:** Settings → Restore purchases. Note that on iOS, App Store consumables (session credits) are not restorable across devices by design — purchases stay on the device they were made on.
- **API key issues:** Check your OpenRouter dashboard at openrouter.ai. Somai needs an active key with credit. Set or replace at Settings → API key.
- **Deleting your data:** Settings → Delete all data. Two-step confirmation required.
