# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-04_

## What Somai is

Somai is a privacy-first mobile app for guided somatic sessions. It is **not** a medical app, **not** a therapy service, **not** a treatment for any condition.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

We do not collect any personal information. We do not run analytics. We do not track your usage. We do not transmit your sessions, transcripts, voice, or camera data anywhere except as noted below.

## Data stored on your device only

The following are stored locally on your device, encrypted, and never leave it:

- Session credit balance (in your device's secure keychain)
- Your OpenRouter API key (in your device's secure keychain)
- Session transcripts and your responses (in an encrypted local database)
- Post-session analysis cards
- App settings

You can delete all of this at any time via Settings → Delete all data.

## What is transmitted off your device

Two transmissions, both initiated by you and only during an active session:

1. **OpenRouter API** — the conversation with the somatic guide is sent to OpenRouter using **your own API key**. Somai has no server. We never see this traffic. OpenRouter's privacy policy applies. Only the current session's conversation context is sent — no past sessions.
2. **ElevenLabs voice (optional)** — if you opt in to premium voice, the guide's spoken text is synthesised by ElevenLabs. Same pattern: direct from your device, with your settings.

The front camera, when enabled, processes frames **entirely on your device** in an isolated process. Camera frames are **never** transmitted, stored, or logged. Only a short text string describing your visual state (eyes open/closed, face active/still) is included in the AI conversation.

## What we do not do

- No analytics SDK
- No remote crash reporting that transmits data
- No advertising network
- No tracking pixels
- No location collection
- No contact list access
- No data sharing with third parties

## Children

Somai is not intended for users under 17.
