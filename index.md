# Somai — Somatic Practice

Somai guides you through structured somatic sessions — quiet, focused practices for noticing what's happening in your body. Each 60–90 minute session uses voice conversation to help you ground, scan, and stay with sensation.

Pay-per-session. No subscription. Your sessions stay on your device.
